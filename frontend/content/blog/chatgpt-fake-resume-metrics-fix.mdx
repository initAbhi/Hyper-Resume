Most developers using ChatGPT to rewrite their resume get trapped by the exact same thing in technical interviews: **fake numbers.**

Tell a generic LLM *"make this backend bullet sound stronger"* and it happily invents:
> *"Reduced API latency by 42% and scaled microservices to 1M+ concurrent users."*

Then you sit across from a Staff Engineer at a product company and they ask:
> *"Cool — how did you benchmark that 42% p95 drop, and what was your load-testing setup for 1M users?"*

Awkward silence. Instant loss of trust.

## 1. Why Prompt Engineering Alone Fails

When building HypeResume, we tested `"Do not invent any metrics or percentages"` inside system prompts across multiple LLMs. Even with strict instructions, creative rewriting still hallucinated unverified numbers **~15–20% of the time**.

Why? Because LLMs optimize for plausible-sounding resume patterns—and in resume corpora, strong bullets almost always contain `%`, `$`, `ms`, or `x`.

<Callout type="info" title="How HypeResume Guards Truth in Code">
Instead of trusting prompts alone, our backend runs a deterministic `validateAndSanitizeMetrics()` pass *outside* the LLM. It extracts every `%`, `$`, `ms`, `k/m/b`, multiplier (`3x`), and scale phrase (`doubled`, `millions of`) from the rewritten bullet and diffs them against your original source text. Any unverified number is automatically stripped and replaced with `[Confirm measurable impact]` so **you** decide what is true before exporting.
</Callout>

## 3. A Simple 3-Part Formula When You Don't Have Metrics

If your current company doesn't share revenue or p95 telemetry with junior/mid-level engineers, structure your bullets around:
1. **Action + Exact Component:** What did you actually own? (`Refactored unindexed PostgreSQL queries`, `Parallelized GitHub Actions CI`)
2. **Mechanism / Tradeoff:** How did you solve it? (`using composite B-tree indexes`, `with Docker layer caching`)
3. **Observable Outcome:** What changed for the team or user? (`eliminated timeout errors during peak sync`, `cut PR feedback wait time`)

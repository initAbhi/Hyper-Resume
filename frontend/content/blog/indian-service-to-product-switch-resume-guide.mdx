If you have 2–5 years of experience at a large Indian IT services firm and are trying to switch to a product company or GCC, you are probably dealing with three specific hurdles:
1. Your day job involves internal tools, support tickets, or legacy enterprise stacks.
2. Client NDAs mean you don't have clean business metrics (`"increased revenue by X%"`).
3. You are wasting precious 1-page real estate on **"The Indian Resume Tax."**

Here is how to fix your resume honestly without fabricating experience.

## 1. Remove "The Indian Resume Tax" Immediately

Product companies and GCCs (Stripe, Atlassian, Razorpay, Swiggy, Flipkart, Goldman Sachs) do not want personal PII cluttering your resume. Strip these out to reclaim ~20% of your page:
- Passport-size photo, Date of Birth, Gender, Marital Status, Father&apos;s Name
- `"Declaration: I hereby declare that all information above is true..."`
- `"Notice Period: 90 Days"` (handle this on the recruiter call, not in your resume header)

## 2. Translate Support & Maintenance Work Into Engineering Ownership

Even if you worked on an enterprise maintenance or banking integration project, you still solved real engineering problems—you just need to frame the **technical mechanism** instead of generic ticket counts.

## 3. Stop Listing Uncorroborated Skills

Listing `Kafka`, `Kubernetes`, and `Redis` in your Skills section when none of your bullets mention them is the #1 reason product-company hiring managers pass on service-company resumes.

Instead, verify every JD requirement against **verbatim bullet evidence**:
- **Supported:** Explicitly proved in your Work Experience or Projects bullets.
- **Needs Confirmation:** Sitting only in your Skills bar with zero bullet proof.
- **Not Evidenced:** Missing gap—build a real, deployed project to bridge it before applying.

## 4. Pair Production Maturity With 1–2 Deployed Target-Stack Projects

If your current company only uses legacy tech, don&apos;t lie about using Go or Next.js at work. Instead:
- Keep your work history 100% truthful (highlighting API design, SQL tuning, CI/CD, or on-call debugging).
- Add a **Projects** section with 1–2 deployed engineering projects (Dockerized backend, auth, schema migrations, live URL) in your target stack—never YouTube tutorial clones like "Netflix Clone" or "To-Do App."

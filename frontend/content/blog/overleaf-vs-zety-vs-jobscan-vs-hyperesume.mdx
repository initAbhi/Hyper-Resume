## 2. When Overleaf Is Still the Best Choice

Let&apos;s be honest: **Overleaf + Jake&apos;s Resume** is legendary on `r/developersIndia` for a reason. If you already know LaTeX, maintain one master resume, and don&apos;t need job-description evidence matching or `.docx` exports for recruiters, Overleaf is completely free and produces rock-solid PDFs.

Where Overleaf falls short is **application velocity**: tailoring bullets per JD without inventing metrics, exporting `.docx` for staffing agencies, and practicing mock interviews based on your exact resume claims.

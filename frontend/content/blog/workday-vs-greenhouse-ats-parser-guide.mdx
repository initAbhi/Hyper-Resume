Every developer on `r/developersIndia` or `r/resumes` has heard the same terrifying claim: *"ATS bots automatically reject 75% of resumes before a human ever sees them."*

That statistic is a 2012 sales myth. Here is what enterprise Applicant Tracking Systems—**Workday, Greenhouse, and Lever**—actually do under the hood, and why chasing a fake "95% ATS score" often ruins good engineering resumes.

## 1. What ATS Platforms Actually Do: Parse → Structure → Rank

Most modern ATS tools do not auto-reject your application based on an arbitrary percentage score. Instead, they run a three-step pipeline:

1. **Text Extraction:** A PDF/DOCX parser extracts raw UTF-8 character streams and bounding-box coordinates from your file.
2. **Field Mapping:** Regex and NLP heuristics map blocks into structured database fields (`Full Name`, `Email`, `Company`, `Title`, `Dates`, `Skills`).
3. **Search & Ranking:** When 800+ engineers apply to one opening, recruiters filter by knockout questions (location, visa, YOE) and review the top-ranked or earliest clean profiles.

<Callout type="warning" title="Where Candidates Actually Lose Out">
If your PDF fails at **Step 1 (Text Extraction)** or **Step 2 (Field Mapping)**, your Workday profile parses blank or scrambled. You don't get "auto-rejected"—you simply become invisible in recruiter search.
</Callout>

## 2. Workday vs Greenhouse vs Lever: Parser Differences

| ATS Platform | Parsing Behavior | Common Failure Mode |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Workday** | Strict field-mapper used by large enterprises & GCCs | 2-column Canva/Figma PDFs scramble left-to-right across rows |
| **Greenhouse** | Clean HTML/PDF preview + structured keyword search | Bloated `>5 MB` image-heavy PDFs or flattened screenshots fail preview |
| **Lever** | Fast parser + recruiter-first inline resume viewer | Custom icon fonts or non-standard section headers (`"My Journey"` instead of `"Experience"`) |

## 3. Why Chasing a "90% Match Score" Backfires

Some legacy ATS scanners give you a single `0–100%` number and tell you to jam missing keywords anywhere to hit `90%`. That works for gaming a naive string counter, but fails the moment a **Staff Engineer or Hiring Manager** reads your resume.

Instead of stuffing keywords into a Skills bar, prove them inside your experience bullets—without inventing fake numbers:

## 4. The 4-Point ATS Engineering Checklist

Before you submit your next application:
- **Use a single-column layout** (like `Jake's Resume`) so left-to-right text extraction never merges unrelated sections.
- **Keep file size under 2 MB** while preserving selectable UTF-8 text (never use online compressors that rasterize pages into images).
- **Use standard section headings:** `Experience`, `Projects`, `Education`, `Skills`.
- **Verify raw text extraction:** Every character in your exported PDF should be cleanly selectable.

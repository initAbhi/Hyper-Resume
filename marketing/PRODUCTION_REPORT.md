## 1. Executive Summary & Creative Vision

The mission was to produce an elite, Silicon Valley agency-grade product demonstration video that escapes standard "screencast" tropes and achieves the visual fidelity of an **Apple Keynote, Linear launch, or Stripe product showcase**.

### Creative Anchors:
- **Hook-to-Climax Narrative:** Directly attacks candidate anxiety (75% ATS rejection rates) within 3 seconds, shows live mathematical proof (5-dimension audit), provides 1-click remediation, and finishes with verifiable vector artifacts (100% extractable PDF + Jake's Resume LaTeX).
- **Cinematic Framing & Smooth Physics:** No random clicking or erratic camera jumping. Every pan, zoom, and 3D cube rotation is locked to candidate attention vectors.
- **Mastered Multi-Track Sound:** Studio-synthesized voiceover synchronized to each UI action, paired with an ambient tech pad bed (-22dB) with smooth edge ducking.
